# Security Owner

Starter role. Every ownership field in the program points at a role id, so this
one exists to make the seeded documents resolve from the first run.

Rename it to match your organization, or replace it with your own roles and
update what references them. Who currently holds the role is not recorded here —
that is the HR system's truth, reachable through `source_of_truth`.
